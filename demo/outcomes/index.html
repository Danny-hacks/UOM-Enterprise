The route to the Bar

One degree, two jurisdictions.

Whatever your starting point, here is how UoME law programmes connect to practice in England & Wales and in Mauritius.

01 · Study at UoME

LLB or GDL

LLB (Hons) with English & Mauritian Law or English Law; or the Graduate Diploma in Law if your first degree is in another subject.

02 · Professional training

Choose your jurisdiction

England & WalesBar Professional Course (barristers) or Legal Practice Course (solicitors)
MauritiusCVLE vocational examination
03 · Practise

Barrister, solicitor or attorney

Over 100 UoME LLB and GDL graduates are now practising Barristers and Attorneys.

Careers support

Help that goes beyond the classroom.

01

One-to-one counselling

The marketing and recruitment team guides prospective undergraduate and postgraduate students towards the right pathway.

02

Employability built into the LLB

Professional Skills and Employability modules run in Years 1, 2 and 3, with moots, debates and seminar advocacy.

03

Alumni who mentor

Alumni return for guest lectures, networking sessions, Induction Week and the annual Alumni Event, and counsel current students on career paths.

04

Fairs, taster lectures & open days

UoME meets students at career fairs, education fairs, road shows and free taster lectures.

05

Student-led clubs

The Law Society and the Rotaract Club organise community, cultural and social activities.

06

Professional recognition

APM, CIOB and IDM accreditation on selected postgraduate courses; CVLE recognition for the LLB and GDL.
