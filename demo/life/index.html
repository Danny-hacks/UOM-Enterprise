Campus & community

Life at UoME.

A compact, connected campus in the heart of Ebene — and a community of students, graduates and academics who look out for each other.

Campus tour

Walk the campus, scroll by scroll.

The Core Building
01

Arrive in Ebene

UoME is on the first floor of The Core Building, at the doorstep of Ebene Cybercity — the financial, banking and BPO-ICT hub of Mauritius — and just opposite the Ebene Commercial Centre.

02

Step inside

Within walking distance you’ll find a practical shopping mall with small shops, a pharmacy and a food court with modern restaurants and cafés. The metro station is about ten minutes away on foot.

03

Learn in modern classrooms

State-of-the-art classrooms with modern equipment. Teaching is led by experienced academics and practitioners, supported on every module by the University’s virtual learning environment.

04

Work in the computer laboratory

A computer laboratory with around 30 high-performance computers gives access to the University of Lancashire’s online resources — e-books, e-journals, e-databases, e-newspapers and a research repository. The library holds textbooks for every module, many available on short-term loan.

05

Get advice on tap

One-to-one counselling for prospective students, and a student support team for everything from assignment extensions to Blackboard.

06

Take a break, join a club

A cosy lunchroom with a big smart TV, with board games available on request. Student-led clubs — the Law Society and the Rotaract Club — organise community, social and cultural activities.

Clubs & societies

Student-led, community-minded.

Two student-led clubs bring the cohort together: the Law Society and the Rotaract Club. Members organise diverse activities to help the community and address important social issues, alongside cultural activities and competitions that contribute to a fun and positive student experience.
