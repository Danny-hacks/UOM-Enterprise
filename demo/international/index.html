Your journey to Mauritius

Seven steps. No surprises.

Select a step to see what happens and what we need from you.

Step 1 of 7

Download the application form

Request the form by email or download it from the Apply page, together with the international checklist.

  • Complete every section of the form
  • Write your personal statement on why you want the course
  • Sign and date the form

Step 2 of 7

Gather your documents

Prepare copies of your academic certificates and transcripts from high school onwards.

  • Copy of your passport and birth certificate
  • One reference letter (undergraduate) or two (postgraduate)
  • Passport photographs: two (undergraduate) or one (postgraduate)

Step 3 of 7

Email your application

Send your application and supporting documents to the admissions office.

  • contactus@uomenterprise.ac.mu
  • International students are not required to pay the Rs 1,000 application fee

Step 4 of 7

Receive your letter of offer

If you are selected for the programme, you will receive a letter of offer within one week of a complete application.

  • Review the programme, fees and payment details in your offer

Step 5 of 7

Accept & make your first payment

Confirm your seat with a first payment of 50% of annual or total course fees, by bank transfer to the State Bank of Mauritius.

  • Fees are published in GBP
  • A 5% discount applies if full fees are paid on enrolment

Step 6 of 7

We process your student visa

Once your first payment is received, UoME applies for the student visa that lets you travel to Mauritius.

  • Passport copy and acceptance letter
  • Sponsor letter confirming financial responsibility
  • Proof of sufficient funds
  • Accommodation must be secured first

Step 7 of 7

Arrive & complete your medical

We can arrange airport pick-up. Within one month of arrival, complete the medical check required by the Passport and Immigration Office.

  • HIV and Hepatitis B tests and a chest X-ray at any private laboratory, clinic or hospital in Mauritius
  • Email your flight details at least five days before you travel

Student visa

We handle the visa process with you.

Once you notify us of your acceptance and your first payment is received, UoME applies for your student visa to enter Mauritius.

What’s required: copies of your passport, your acceptance letter, a sponsor letter confirming financial responsibility, and proof of sufficient funds. A student visa cannot be processed until accommodation has been secured.

How long it lasts: a minimum of one year, renewable every year provided you remain registered with UoME the following year.

Accommodation & arrival

A place to live before you land.

UoME works with dedicated landlords who offer a range of private accommodation at reasonable prices. We send you the available options with prices and photos so you can choose — and you may share an apartment or house with other students. We can help with your negotiations with the landlord and with the lease agreement.

Airport pick-up. We can arrange for you to be collected from the airport and delivered safely to your accommodation. Email us your flight details at least five days before travel (taxi fare payable by the student).

Living in Mauritius

An island that welcomes the world.

Mauritius sits in the middle of the Indian Ocean, about 2,400 km off the east coast of Africa. Over 150 kilometres of white sandy beaches surround it, and its lagoons are protected by the world’s third largest coral reef.

The country is multi-ethnic and multicultural, with a population of about 1.2 million. Its government system is closely modelled on the British Westminster parliamentary system, and the country is highly ranked for democracy and for economic and political freedom. The capital is Port Louis.

FAQs

Moving to Mauritius: your questions.

All FAQs

Do I need a visa?

International students need a student visa. UoME applies for it after you accept your offer and the first payment is received. You will need your passport, acceptance letter, a sponsor letter confirming financial responsibility and proof of sufficient funds. Accommodation must be secured first.

Is accommodation provided?

UoME works with dedicated landlords and provides lists of private accommodation (with prices and photos) in Ebene, Quatre Bornes and Rose-Hill. You can share a home with other students, and UoME helps with negotiations and the lease.

How much does it cost to live in Mauritius?

The yearly cost of living is estimated at around £3,500. Try the cost-of-living calculator on the International page. Students also benefit from free bus transport to and from the university.

Is there a medical requirement?

Yes. Within one month of arrival you need an HIV and Hepatitis B test and a chest X-ray at a private laboratory, clinic or hospital in Mauritius.
