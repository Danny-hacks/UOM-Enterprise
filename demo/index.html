FAQs

Quick answers, before you ask.

The questions we hear most from applicants and parents — answered from UoME’s published information.

All FAQs Talk to a person

How do I apply?

Complete the application form (online, or download the Word form), attach your documents and personal statement, and submit it. Local applicants can submit in person or by email and pay the Rs 1,000 application fee by bank transfer. International applicants submit by email and do not pay the fee.

What are the entry requirements?

Undergraduate: 3 A-levels and 1 Subsidiary (or equivalent) or 112 UCAS points, with Grade C in English at O/SC level or IELTS 6.0. Graduate and postgraduate: a bachelor degree at 2:2 or above (or relevant professional experience for some programmes), with English at Grade C or IELTS 6.0–6.5. UoME applies a flexible admissions policy and considers each applicant as an individual.

Can I study while working?

Yes. The MSc and LLM programmes are part-time over 1.5 years, delivered in a hybrid mix of online and on-campus study, so professionals can keep working.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Tuition is paid in three instalments — the first on acceptance of your offer, the others on published dates for your intake.

Is my degree recognised?

UoME is registered with the Higher Education Commission, all programmes are HEC-accredited, and University of Lancashire courses delivered in Mauritius are recognised under the UK system regulated by the QAA. The LLB and GDL are recognised by the CVLE; selected MSc programmes are accredited by APM, CIOB or IDM.

Do I need a visa?

International students need a student visa. UoME applies for it after you accept your offer and the first payment is received. You will need your passport, acceptance letter, a sponsor letter confirming financial responsibility and proof of sufficient funds. Accommodation must be secured first.
