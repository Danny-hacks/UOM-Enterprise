Frequently asked questions.

Applying, fees, studying, international students and campus life — answered from UoME’s published information.

Can’t find it?
Call (230) 467 8925, WhatsApp us or contact us.

Applying

How do I apply?

Complete the application form (online, or download the Word form), attach your documents and personal statement, and submit it. Local applicants can submit in person or by email and pay the Rs 1,000 application fee by bank transfer. International applicants submit by email and do not pay the fee.

What documents do I need?

Certificates and transcripts (O/SC, A-level/HSC or equivalent, and degree certificates where applicable), proof of identity, a personal statement, one reference (undergraduate) or two (postgraduate), and passport-size photographs. Local applicants also need proof of address; international applicants a copy of their passport. Bring originals for verification.

How long until I hear back?

Selected applicants receive a letter of offer within 3 working days of a complete application (within 1 week for international applicants). To accept, email student support, then make the first payment of 50% of annual or total fees to confirm your seat.

What are the entry requirements?

Undergraduate: 3 A-levels and 1 Subsidiary (or equivalent) or 112 UCAS points, with Grade C in English at O/SC level or IELTS 6.0. Graduate and postgraduate: a bachelor degree at 2:2 or above (or relevant professional experience for some programmes), with English at Grade C or IELTS 6.0–6.5. UoME applies a flexible admissions policy and considers each applicant as an individual.

Can I apply without a law degree?

Yes. The Graduate Diploma in Law is a conversion course for graduates of any subject with a 2:2 or above, and for those with an overseas law degree. The LLM in Financial & Commercial Law also welcomes non-law graduates.

Fees

How much are the fees?

Fees are published per programme in Mauritian rupees for local students and pounds sterling for international students. Use the fees estimator on the Apply page to see your programme and intake.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Tuition is paid in three instalments — the first on acceptance of your offer, the others on published dates for your intake.

Is there a discount for paying early?

Yes. Local students who pay full fees by the early-payment deadline for their intake receive a 5% discount, and international students receive 5% when full fees are paid on enrolment.

What is the refund policy?

100% before the start of the semester, 70% within two weeks of the start, 50% between three and eight weeks, and no refund after eight weeks.

Studying

Can I study while working?

Yes. The MSc and LLM programmes are part-time over 1.5 years, delivered in a hybrid mix of online and on-campus study, so professionals can keep working.

Is my degree recognised?

UoME is registered with the Higher Education Commission, all programmes are HEC-accredited, and University of Lancashire courses delivered in Mauritius are recognised under the UK system regulated by the QAA. The LLB and GDL are recognised by the CVLE; selected MSc programmes are accredited by APM, CIOB or IDM.

Can I practise law after the LLB?

The LLB (Hons) with English & Mauritian Law is a Qualifying Law Degree for England & Wales and Mauritius. Next comes the Bar Professional Course or Legal Practice Course in England & Wales, or the CVLE vocational examination in Mauritius.

What support is available?

A student support team helps with extensions, mitigating circumstances, enrolment, Blackboard and complaints, and a finance team handles payments. Students also have a library, computer laboratory and online resources from the University of Lancashire.

International

Do I need a visa?

International students need a student visa. UoME applies for it after you accept your offer and the first payment is received. You will need your passport, acceptance letter, a sponsor letter confirming financial responsibility and proof of sufficient funds. Accommodation must be secured first.

Is accommodation provided?

UoME works with dedicated landlords and provides lists of private accommodation (with prices and photos) in Ebene, Quatre Bornes and Rose-Hill. You can share a home with other students, and UoME helps with negotiations and the lease.

How much does it cost to live in Mauritius?

The yearly cost of living is estimated at around £3,500. Try the cost-of-living calculator on the International page. Students also benefit from free bus transport to and from the university.

Is there a medical requirement?

Yes. Within one month of arrival you need an HIV and Hepatitis B test and a chest X-ray at a private laboratory, clinic or hospital in Mauritius.

Campus

Where is the campus?

On the first floor of The Core Building in Ebene Cybercity, opposite the Ebene Commercial Centre. A mall, pharmacy and food court are within walking distance and the metro station is about ten minutes away on foot.

Can I visit before applying?

Yes. The team offers open days, free taster lectures and one-to-one counselling. Book a visit or call (230) 467 8925 between 09:00 and 16:30, Monday to Friday, including lunchtime.

Which clubs and societies are there?

The student-led Law Society and Rotaract Club organise community, social and cultural activities and competitions.
