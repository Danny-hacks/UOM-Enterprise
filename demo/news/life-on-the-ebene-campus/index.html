Life on the Ebene campus: what is actually there

Classrooms, a computer lab, a library, a lunchroom — and a metro station ten minutes away.

Key takeaways
  • The campus is on the first floor of The Core Building in Ebene Cybercity.
  • A mall, pharmacy and food court are within walking distance; the metro is about ten minutes away.
  • The Law Society and Rotaract Club are student-led.

Where you will be

UoME’s campus is on the first floor of The Core Building in Ebene, at the doorstep of Ebene Cybercity — the financial, banking and BPO-ICT hub of Mauritius — and opposite the Ebene Commercial Centre. A mall with a pharmacy, shops and a food court is within walking distance, and the metro station is about ten minutes away on foot.

Inside the campus

You will find state-of-the-art classrooms, a computer laboratory of around 30 high-performance computers, a library of textbooks (many available on short-term loan), and a cosy lunchroom with a big smart TV and board games on request.

Online resources

University of Lancashire’s Learning Information Services provides e-books, e-journals, e-databases A–Z, e-images, e-newspapers and a research repository, licensed for educational use.

Clubs and community

Student-led clubs — the Law Society and the Rotaract Club — organise community, social and cultural activities throughout the year.
