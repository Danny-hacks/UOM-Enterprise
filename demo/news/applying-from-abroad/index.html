Applying from abroad: your seven steps to Mauritius

From application form to student visa — the process international applicants follow, in plain English.

Key takeaways
  • International applicants do not pay the Rs 1,000 application fee.
  • Your student visa is processed after your first payment — and only once accommodation is secured.
  • You need a short medical check within one month of arriving.

Your application

International applicants download the application form and checklist, complete every section (including a personal statement), attach certificates and transcripts, and provide references — one for an undergraduate course, two for a postgraduate course. Applications are signed, dated and emailed to the admissions office. International students are not required to pay the Rs 1,000 application fee.

Offer, payment and visa

If you are selected you receive a letter of offer within one week of a complete application. When you confirm your acceptance and the first payment of the course fee is received, UoME applies for your student visa to enter Mauritius. The visa lasts a minimum of one year and is renewable each year while you remain registered.

Accommodation

A student visa cannot be processed until accommodation has been secured. UoME provides lists of private accommodation from dedicated landlords in Ebene, Quatre Bornes and Rose-Hill, with prices and photos, and can help with negotiations and the lease.

On arrival

Within one month you will need a medical check — HIV and Hepatitis B tests and a chest X-ray at a private laboratory, clinic or hospital in Mauritius. UoME can also arrange airport pick-up if you email your flight details at least five days before travelling (taxi fare payable by the student).
