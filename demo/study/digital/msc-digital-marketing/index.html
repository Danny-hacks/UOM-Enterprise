Overview

Plan, deliver and measure in a digital-first world.

Our MSc Digital Marketing was created with digital marketing specialists and agency partners. It helps you plan, deliver and assess marketing activity in a digital-first world. You learn how to understand online consumer behaviour and use data and analytics responsibly, and you design integrated digital marketing campaigns that deliver clear, measurable results.

You gain cutting-edge knowledge in digital and social media marketing, with both theoretical and applied perspectives, and the ability to apply a range of specialist digital marketing skills inside the organisation in which you work.

Careers & next steps

Where it can take you.

  • Digital strategy and online brand communications
  • Data-driven marketing and analytics
  • Social media and digital campaign management
  • Marketing leadership in organisations of any size
In 2026 UoME celebrates the first graduating cohort of the MSc Digital Marketing, preparing graduates for careers in digital strategy, online branding and communications, data-driven marketing, social media and digital campaign management.

Accreditation & recognition

Institute of Data & Marketing (IDM). The MSc Digital Marketing offers the opportunity to gain certification from the IDM, the accrediting body of the Data & Marketing Association (DMA).

Entry requirements

Who can apply.

  • An honours degree with 2:2 classification or higher
  • English: Grade C at O/SC level, or IELTS 6.0 – 6.5
  • An honours degree with 2:2 classification or higher
  • English language competence: IELTS 6.5 (or equivalent)

Appropriate professional experience is also considered for postgraduate applications.

How to apply

Four steps to your offer.

01

Submit your application

Complete the application form with your personal statement and certificates, with two referees. Local applicants pay the Rs 1,000 application fee; it is waived for international students.

02

Receive your offer

If selected, you receive a letter of offer within 3 working days of a complete application (within 1 week for international applicants).

03

Accept & confirm your seat

Email studentsupport@uomenterprise.ac.mu to accept, then make the first payment — 50% of annual or total course fees — to confirm your place.

04

Begin your studies

International students: your student visa is processed once the first payment is received. Welcome to UoME.
