Overview

Build careers as well as buildings.

The MSc in Construction Project Management is designed to develop the knowledge and skills of construction professionals in the context of contemporary construction projects. There is a focus on construction project management knowledge areas, including the planning and control methods within the project management process, and you extend your knowledge of technologies for the resolution of new-build problems.

The programme gives you opportunities to develop the personal, interpersonal and project-personnel skills relevant to your working environment, in the context of international construction project management.

Programme structure

What you’ll study.

Course outline

  • Project Planning, Control and Analysis
  • Risk and Value Management
  • Project Team and Leadership Development
  • Strategic Project Management
  • Health and Safety Management
  • Advanced Construction Technology
  • Dissertation (triple module)

How you’re assessed

Coursework and applied assignments, culminating in a dissertation.

Careers & next steps

Where it can take you.

  • Construction project management and site delivery leadership
  • Planning, cost and risk control on contemporary construction projects
  • Engineering and built-environment careers — UoME engineering alumni are contributing to Mauritius’s construction industry
  • Progression towards APM and CIOB professional recognition
Graduates in engineering have played key roles in design, project supervision and technical sales in Mauritius’s built environment (terms and conditions apply to accreditation).

Accreditation & recognition

Association for Project Management (APM). The MSc Project Management and the MSc Construction Project Management are accredited by the APM.

Chartered Institute of Building (CIOB). The MSc Construction Project Management is also accredited by the CIOB.

Entry requirements

Who can apply.

  • An honours degree with 2:2 classification or higher in a construction or engineering discipline that demonstrates awareness of construction processes and procedures
  • English: Grade C at O/SC level, or IELTS 6.0 – 6.5
  • An honours degree with 2:2 classification or higher in a construction or engineering discipline that demonstrates awareness of construction processes and procedures
  • English language competence: IELTS 6.0 (or equivalent)

Appropriate professional experience is also considered for postgraduate applications.

How to apply

Four steps to your offer.

01

Submit your application

Complete the application form with your personal statement and certificates, with two referees. Local applicants pay the Rs 1,000 application fee; it is waived for international students.

02

Receive your offer

If selected, you receive a letter of offer within 3 working days of a complete application (within 1 week for international applicants).

03

Accept & confirm your seat

Email studentsupport@uomenterprise.ac.mu to accept, then make the first payment — 50% of annual or total course fees — to confirm your place.

04

Begin your studies

International students: your student visa is processed once the first payment is received. Welcome to UoME.
