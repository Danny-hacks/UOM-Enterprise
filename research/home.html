Over 900 alumni making an impact

With more than 900 graduates, the institution has developed a strong and influential alumni network across Mauritius and beyond. Graduates have built successful careers in Law and public service, Government and policy making, Engineering and construction, Business and corporate sectors.

This growing alumni community creates valuable networking and career opportunities for current students.

Leading name for Law education in Mauritius
Renowned for excellence in Law, UOM Enterprise counts more than 100 alumni who are practicing as barristers and attorneys. The dual-qualified LLB (Hons) with English & Mauritian Law equips students for legal practice both locally and in the UK. The LLB courses prepares students with the academic and analytical foundation needed for legal practice and further professional training.

Alumni in Governance and Public Service
The institution’s alumni include the former President of the Republic and the current ICT Minister, demonstrating its capacity to nurture leaders who shape national policy and governance.

Engineering alumni driving the Local Construction Industry
Graduates in engineering have made significant contributions across engineering design, project supervision, and technical sales, playing key roles in the development of Mauritius’ built environment.

Future-focused courses for the Digital Economy

In 2026, the institution will celebrate the first graduating cohort of the MSc Digital Marketing course, preparing graduates for careers in digital strategy, online branding and communications, data-driven marketing, social media and digital campaign management.
